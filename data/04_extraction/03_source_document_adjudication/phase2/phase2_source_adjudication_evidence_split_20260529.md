## Decision rules applied

- Inclusion requires an educational population/setting and either a correlation matrix or standardized path coefficients with at least two target constructs (`AI_Adoption_MASEM_Coding_Manual_v2.md:82-99`).
- For correlation extraction, use source-reported target-construct correlations; do not use HTMT/CR/AVE/diagonal square-root AVE as construct correlations; use standardized paths only under the beta-to-r rule when no usable correlation matrix is reported (`AI_Adoption_MASEM_Coding_Manual_v2.md:318-340`).
- Perceived risk/risk concern is excluded by default unless the source definition is affective AI anxiety/fear/apprehension (`AI_Adoption_MASEM_Coding_Manual_v2.md:398-405`).
- Full-text/data-extraction exclusion `E-FT1` applies when fewer than two usable construct-pair statistics are available; duplicate samples/sources are excluded rather than double-counted (`AI_Adoption_MASEM_Coding_Manual_v2.md:714-719`).
